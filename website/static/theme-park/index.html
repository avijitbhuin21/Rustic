Themes

Browse themes for Rustic, see each one on a live mock of the editor, and make your own in the browser. Sharing needs no account. A shared theme is public and can't be changed afterwards.

    To use it: download the file, then in Rustic open Settings, Appearance, Themes and choose "Import file". Rustic asks you to review a theme before it applies.
